# Research summary: 22 October 2026

First build of the database. The repository was empty, so there were no existing records to re-check, merge or archive.

## Method

- **Scope:** AI training useful to an experienced senior executive in Ireland across four objectives: enterprise transformation, strategy and advisory work, governance and assurance, and practical engineering.
- **Research:** subject areas were researched in parallel (strategy and sales; governance and security; generative AI and agents; Python, data and ML; RAG, LLMOps and cloud certifications). The maintainer then reviewed the records, made pricing conventions consistent and spot-checked key claims.
- **Verification method:** every record is marked `web-search-of-official-pages`. This environment's network policy blocked direct page fetches, including coursera.org and ecb.europa.eu. Facts were therefore taken from search-engine extracts of official pages, with the official URL recorded as the source. Where an official page did not surface, the fact is either marked `not verified` or attributed to a named third-party source in the record's limitations or gaps. **Re-check prices and dates on the official page before enrolling.**
- **Spot checks:** the maintainer re-confirmed the following against official pages:
  - the Maven price and rating for AI Evals (USD 4,200; 4.7/5 from 1,042 reviews);
  - the IAPP AIGP exam fees (USD 649 member, USD 799 non-member, from the candidate handbook);
  - the Coursera rating for the Machine Learning Specialization.

  The Microsoft AI-102 to AI-103 replacement is confirmed only by third-party sources and is marked low confidence.
- **Currency:** ECB euro reference rates for 21 October 2026 (USD 1.1234, GBP 0.83825 per EUR). These were read from the ECB reference-rate page via search, because the ECB feed could not be fetched directly.

## Verification gaps

- **Study hours** are not published or verified for 24 of 47 records, mostly vendor exams and free courses. Learning-path totals are therefore a floor.
- **EUR cost** is not verified for 11 records. Most are Coursera subscription items: Coursera's monthly price conflicted across sources (USD 49, 59 and 79), so no figure was recorded. Kellogg's fee was seen only on third-party sites. Microsoft AI-901 and AI-103 prices were third-party only.
- **Credential** is not verified for 4 records: Digital Skillnet Leading AI Strategy, Google ML Crash Course, Kaggle AI Agents Intensive (self-paced) and AI Evals (Maven).
- **Low confidence** for 5 records: Kellogg AI Strategies, Microsoft AI-103, IoD Ireland workshop, Trinity AI in Digital Marketing, Digital Skillnet Leading AI Strategy.
- **IoD Ireland board workshop** has status `not verified`. The last listed session (24 September 2026) has passed and no new date is published.
- **Conflicting evidence** is logged per record in `verification.conflicts`. Examples: review counts that differ between snapshots, Oxford's February 2027 fee (GBP 1,750 vs 1,950), the AAIA eligibility window, Google PMLE validity (2 vs 3 years), and Trinity intake dates.
- **Coverage gaps to research next:**

  - Sales and partnerships are thin. The best candidate is Kellogg Mastering Sales (relaunched February 2026), but its fee is not verified.
